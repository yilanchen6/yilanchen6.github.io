Research

I develop algorithms for LLM post-training, focusing on reinforcement learning and on-policy distillation for reasoning models and coding agents. My research combines algorithm design and theoretical analysis with distributed LLM training and empirical evaluation.

My broader research spans machine learning theory, including transformer in-context learning, provable dataset distillation, neural-network generalization, and connections between neural networks and kernel methods.
